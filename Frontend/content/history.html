History of Table Tennis

1880s - Origins

Table tennis began in England as a leisure activity among the upper class, using books as nets and everyday objects as paddles.

1926 - ITTF Foundation

The International Table Tennis Federation was founded, establishing official rules and organizing global competitions.

1950s - Asian Dominance

Asian players revolutionized the sport with new techniques, speed, and spin-based gameplay.

1988 - Olympic Sport

Table tennis became an official Olympic sport, increasing its global recognition and competitiveness.

Modern Era

Today, table tennis is one of the fastest sports in the world, combining strategy, reflexes, and advanced technology.
